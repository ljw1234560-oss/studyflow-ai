About the Service

Why StudyFlow AI?

Many students manage assignments, exams, and projects at the same time. StudyFlow AI is designed to reduce confusion by combining planning, prioritization, and study feedback in one simple web service.

Target Users

University students who need a simple way to manage academic tasks, deadlines, and study priorities.

Core Value

The service helps users decide what to study first by showing deadlines, priorities, and completion progress.

Technology
